Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know before you order
Which areas do you serve?
We serve Johor Bahru (Ulu Tiram), Kuala Lumpur (Subang Jaya) and Singapore — for both printing and signage works.
How do I place an order?
Pick a product, configure your size, material and options to get an instant quote, then submit your details and artwork. Our team confirms the order, you make payment, and we produce and deliver or arrange self-collection.
Can I get a price before ordering?
Yes. Every product page gives you an instant price estimate as you configure it, or you can ask our AI assistant for a quick quote and product recommendation right here on the site.
Do you deliver, or is it self-collect?
Both. You can self-collect at our workshop or have it delivered. On-site installation is available in the JB area for selected products such as lightboxes and large signage.
Can you handle custom designs?
Absolutely. You can upload your own artwork, or use our AI-assisted design tools for custom signboards and LED neon signs.
How long does production take?
Lead time depends on the product type and quantity. We'll confirm the exact turnaround when we review your order — message us on WhatsApp if you have a deadline.
