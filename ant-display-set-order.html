Display Set FAQ

Which display should I choose?
Roll up bunting is the easiest to carry and set up for events, exhibitions and shop entrances. Bunting with stand is the budget option for promotions. Beach flags catch attention from far away, and the jumbo stand works as a large backdrop. For booths and restaurants, choose a pop up table, menu stand or easel stand.
Is printing included in the price?
Yes. Every set includes both the stand and the full-colour printing — just send us your artwork.
Standard or deluxe roll up?
Both have the same 32" × 80" visual size printed on synthetic paper. The standard roll up uses an aluminium base, while the deluxe has a premium heavy-duty base that is sturdier for frequent use. Double-sided versions can be seen from both directions.
What materials are used?
Roll up bunting: synthetic paper. Bunting with stand and jumbo stand: tarpaulin. Beach flags: fabric cloth. Pop up table: white sticker with matt lamination. Menu stand: 3mm foamboard with matt-laminated sticker. Easel stand: 5mm foamboard with matt-laminated sticker.
How long does it take?
Our team contacts you within 1 business day to confirm your artwork and payment. Production usually takes 5–7 working days after confirmation. Need it for an event? Tell us your date in the remarks.
Do you deliver?
Yes. You can self-collect at our workshop in Ulu Tiram, or choose delivery within JB or to Singapore when you place your order.
I don't have artwork yet. Can I still order?
Yes. You can upload AI, PDF, PSD, EPS, JPG or PNG files (up to 50MB each) at checkout, or order now and send your artwork to us later on WhatsApp. The final price is confirmed after we review your artwork.
Where are you located?
Our workshop is at 6, Jalan Puncak, Taman Perindustrian Puncak, 81800 Ulu Tiram, Johor — open Monday–Saturday, 9:00am–6:00pm. Open in Google Maps
