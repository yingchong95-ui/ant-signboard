LED Banner Display FAQ

How is the price calculated?
Price is RM 120 per square foot, based on the panel size (excluding the frame). Red and white are the same price. The price includes the controller and display unit; installation is quoted separately. Enter your size above for an instant estimate. Prices exclude SST.
Why is the size slightly different from what I entered?
LED displays are built from individual P10 panels, each 320 × 160 mm. Your size is rounded to the closest number of whole panels — for example, 4 × 1 ft becomes 4.20 × 1.05 ft (8 panels). The size and price above always show what will actually be made.
Does the size include the frame?
No. The size is the panel size only. The finished display with its aluminium frame will be slightly larger, so please leave some extra space for installation.
Can I change the message later?
Yes. You can change the message anytime from your phone. English and Chinese are both supported, and you can choose scroll left, static or flashing. Try your message in the live preview above.
Red or white — which should I choose?
Both are the same price — they just use different LED modules. Switch between red and white above to see how your message looks in each colour.
How long does it take to make?
Usually 5–7 working days. Need it by a certain date? Tell us in the remarks.
Is installation included?
We install within Johor Bahru. Installation is quoted separately, because it depends on the site's electrical point and the mounting height. WhatsApp us a photo of your site for a quick quote.
Is there a warranty?
Yes. Every LED banner display comes with a 12-month warranty covering the whole display.
Where are you located?
