Signboard FAQ

What is the difference between frontlit, backlit and nonlit signboards?
Frontlit: the letter face lights up, so your name is bright and easy to read at night. Backlit: the light shines from behind the letters onto the wall or panel, creating a soft halo glow. Nonlit: no lighting — clean metal or painted letters, suitable for indoor walls or shops that close before dark.
How much does a shop signboard cost?
Flat panel signboards start from RM 1,400, flat panel with 3D letters from RM 3,000, aluminium strip with 3D letters from RM 3,500, lightbox from RM 2,600 and wall lettering from RM 1,500. Prices include installation. Enter your size in the designer above, and our sales team will send a personalised quotation within 1 business day.
How long does it take to make a signboard?
After your deposit, we finalise the design with you and submit it for DBP (Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka) language approval — about 7 working days. Once approved, production takes about 10–14 working days, so plan for around 17–21 working days in total. Tell us your opening date and we'll plan around it.
Is installation included?
Yes. Our prices include installation. If your signboard is mounted high and needs a skylift, the skylift is charged separately. We install across Johor Bahru, Kuala Lumpur and Singapore.
Do I need a council permit for my signboard?
Most outdoor signboards in Malaysia need a licence from the local council (e.g. MBJB, MBIP, MPKu). We can handle the application for you — the council licence is charged separately (runner fee).
Is there a warranty?
Yes. Our signboards come with a 12-month warranty on colour and LED lighting, and 6 months on the transformer, starting from the installation date. You can check your warranty status anytime on your order tracking page.
I don't have a design yet. Can you help?
Yes. Use the designer above to try your shop name, colours and sign type, or upload your logo for a preview. Our team will then prepare the final design, and production only starts after you confirm it.
Which areas do you serve?
We serve Johor Bahru (Ulu Tiram), Kuala Lumpur (Subang Jaya) and Singapore. Our workshop is at 6, Jalan Puncak, Taman Perindustrian Puncak, 81800 Ulu Tiram, Johor — open Monday–Saturday, 9:00am–6:00pm. Open in Google Maps
